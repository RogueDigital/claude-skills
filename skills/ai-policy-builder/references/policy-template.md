# One-page policy template

Replace every square bracket. Keep the section order. Aim for one page.

```
AI USE AT [COMPANY NAME]
Version 1. Owner: [name]. Review by: [date]

WHY THIS EXISTS
AI tools save us time. This page explains how to use them without
putting customers, colleagues or the company at risk. If something
is not covered here, ask [name] before you go ahead.

1. WHICH TOOLS YOU MAY USE
- [Tool and account type, for example ChatGPT Business], signed in
  with your company email.
- Free or personal accounts of any AI tool are not to be used for
  company work, including on your phone.
- Want to try another tool? Ask [name]. We will answer within [time].

2. WHAT GOES IN
GREEN: public information, your own drafts, general questions.
AMBER: [internal material, customer and supplier contact details].
Approved tools only, and only what the task needs. If it works with
names removed, remove them.
RED: never goes into any AI tool. [Passwords and access codes, bank
and card details, health or HR records, anything under a
confidentiality agreement, anything a client has told us is
confidential, sector-specific items].

3. PERSONAL DATA
We follow UK GDPR. Only put in the personal data the task needs.
Do not use AI tools to make a decision about a person on their own.

4. CLIENT CONFIDENTIAL WORK
[Client work is red unless the contract allows AI. Check with
[name] first.]

5. CHECKING WHAT COMES OUT
- You are responsible for anything you send, whoever or whatever
  drafted it.
- Check every number, name, date and claim against the source.
- [Quotes, contracts, legal or HR wording, advice] are read by
  [name or role] before they go out.
- Never present AI output as expert advice.
- No AI-written message goes to a customer without a person reading
  it first. If a customer is talking to an AI, they are told.

6. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- Pasted something you should not have? Tell [name] the same day.
  Reporting it is never a disciplinary matter. Delay is what makes
  it worse.
- Output that looks wrong? Tell [name] so we can fix it for everyone.

7. WHO TO ASK
[Name], [email or channel].

[REGULATED WORK: one line saying the regulator's rules come first.
Delete if not needed.]
```

## Notes for the writer

- Section 3 and 4 can be merged into section 2 if the page runs long.
- Do not add a section for things that do not apply.
- The review date is usually six months out. Put the owner's name next to it.
